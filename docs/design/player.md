# Player

The player on both platforms. Behaviour rules are in `guardrails.md` (Playback); this file is the layout and look. The interactive mockups (controls, scrubbing, skip offer, up next, Settings → Playback) are the `PlayerControls` and `PlayerScreen` components of the VibeyFin design-system artifact (the Settings screens are `SettingsPhone` and `SettingsTablet`, see `screens.md`): https://claude.ai/artifact/L3iSAKqvpmXtUtRy2qBGAe

## iOS (SwiftUI, AVPlayer or Aether)

The engine under the controls is chosen per title and does not change the layout; see `docs/ios-playback.md`.

Apple's scheme: **white on Liquid Glass, never copper.** Copper is only the scrubber's progress. iOS 26 uses `.buttonStyle(.glass)` / `.glassEffect()` in one `GlassEffectContainer`; before iOS 26 the same controls sit on a `scrim` disc. Symbols are `.primary`.

- **Top bar**, one baseline, 44pt cells, top-aligned:
  - Leading: a round glass `xmark`.
  - Title (`.headline`) and one meta line (`.footnote`, secondary): episode, then the format badge as plain text ("S2:E4 · Northbound · 4K · HDR10"). The badge is no longer a capsule.
  - Trailing: **one** glass capsule holding, in order, next episode (episodes with a next one only), speed (`1×`, a menu), audio and subtitles (`captions.bubble`, `.fill` when a subtitle is on, a menu), picture in picture, AirPlay (`AVRoutePickerView`). No control floats on its own.
- **AirPlay active** (`AVPlayer.isExternalPlaybackActive`, video AirPlay only; audio-only AirPlay to a HomePod or Sonos keeps the normal player): the picture is on the other screen, so the phone or iPad keeps the same controls over the item's artwork (backdrop image, else poster) blurred and dimmed on the `scrim`; with no artwork, a flat dark ground (the secondary system background). A status pill sits above the transport: the `airplay.video` symbol and "Playing on <device>" (the audio route's port name; "Playing on AirPlay" when the system gives none), one line cut with an ellipsis, white on glass (a `scrim-strong` disc before iOS 26), never copper, not tappable. It stays while paused or stalled. The AirPlay button shows pressed and opens the system picker to switch device or stop. **The PiP button is hidden** while AirPlay is active, because PiP does not apply to external playback; it returns when AirPlay stops. The controls never auto-hide while AirPlaying, the volume and brightness swipes are off (the hardware buttons still work), the screen is not kept awake, and the phone stays landscape. Starting or stopping crossfades in 0.25 s (none with Reduce Motion) and the position does not change; the skip offer and up next keep their corner. The receiver's own UI (Apple TV, TV player) is the system's; the app can't style it. Switching audio or subtitles keeps the position (issue #47). Mockups: `PlayerControls`, `PlayerPhone`, `PlayerTablet`.
- **Transport**, centered: `gobackward.<n>`, play / pause, `goforward.<n>` as glass discs of 56 / 76 / 56pt at the default text size (they scale with Dynamic Type). `n` comes from Settings → Playback (default 10 back, 10 forward).
- **Bottom:** scrubber with copper progress, no thumb, thickening to 10pt while dragged; elapsed left, remaining right (`-12:30`), tabular numerals; trickplay thumbnail and timestamp above the bar while dragging. The **buffered segment** is white at 40% opacity (`overlay-strong`) between the copper fill and the track, ending at the loaded position (`loadedTimeRanges`).
- **Stalled:** while the player waits for data a white `ProgressView` replaces the three transport buttons, centred; the top bar and scrubber stay.
- **Skip total:** after a skip, a "10s" capsule on the `scrim` (`.title3`, semibold, tabular numerals) by the side the video moved, holding the running total of skips in a row (`skipRun`); it fades in and out and is hidden from VoiceOver.
- **Skip offer** ("Skip Intro", "Skip Recap", "Skip Credits"): a white glass capsule with a `forward.end` symbol, bottom-right above the scrubber. Shown for the whole segment, also with the controls hidden (then it is the only thing on screen, over a faint bottom scrim).
- **Next episode:** for an episode with a next one only (never a movie or the last episode), a `forward.end.fill` button, first in the top bar's glass capsule (before speed, audio and subtitles, PiP and AirPlay). It plays the next episode now, like Play Now on the up next card, and gives way to that card while it shows. VoiceOver: "Next episode", the episode's label and title as the hint. Mockup: `PlayerNextEpisode`.
- **Swipe for volume and brightness** (issue #40, as on Android): a vertical swipe in the right quarter of the width sets the volume, in the left quarter the brightness, and the middle half does nothing (on iPhone and iPad alike); up raises, a full sweep is 60% of the height, drags starting within 40 pt of the top or bottom or 24 pt of the sides (Notification Centre, Control Centre, the home indicator) or going sideways first are ignored. iOS has no call that sets the volume, so the swipe moves a hidden `MPVolumeView`'s slider; that view being on screen also hides the system volume HUD, so the hardware buttons show the same capsule while the player is up. iOS has no per-window brightness: the screen's brightness from before the first swipe comes back when the player closes or the app leaves the foreground. The indicator is a Liquid Glass capsule (52 × 160 pt, a `scrim` capsule before iOS 26) on the swiped side, filled from the bottom in white like Control Center, with `speaker.slash.fill` / `speaker.wave.1…3.fill` or `sun.min.fill` / `sun.max.fill` at its foot, black once the fill covers it. Never copper. It fades out 1 s after the finger lifts; VoiceOver hears the level when it does. Off with the same per-profile switch as Android.
- **Up next:** takes the skip offer's corner. A glass card (`radius-ios-card`): the next episode's thumbnail with a seconds-left badge, "Up Next", the title, a **white filled** "Play Now" capsule and a glass "Watch Credits", both 44 pt tall and equally wide. It appears when the Outro starts (the last 30 s without one), in place of Skip Credits, and its countdown pauses while the video is paused.

## Android (Compose, Media3)

M3 on the scrim, no glass. Top app bar: `←`, title and meta line, speed, audio and subtitles. Transport: three clickable 48 dp buttons, the two skip buttons a generic arrow glyph with the profile's seconds drawn as text over it (not Material `replay_10` `IconButton`s, which exist for a few values only), and play / pause on a `scrim-strong` disc, all `ink` on the scrim. A press turns a skip arrow about 30° the way it points and springs it back. After any skip, the side the video moved shows Android's own double-tap seek feedback (`SeekRipple.kt`): a light half circle (`ink` at 12%) curving in from that edge over about 38% of the width, a ripple for each tap, three chevrons lighting up in turn in the skip direction and the running total ("30s") under them. It takes no touches and is hidden from TalkBack. Scrubber: M3 look with a bar handle and a buffered segment (white at 40% opacity (`overlay-strong`), up to `player.bufferedPosition`) between the copper fill and the track. While stalled, a white `CircularProgressIndicator` replaces the three transport buttons. Skip offer: a tonal button (`accent-soft`). Up next: a `surface-2` card with a filled "Play now" and a tonal "Watch credits", both 44 dp tall and equally wide. It shows when the Outro starts, in place of Skip credits, and its countdown pauses while the video is paused.

**Next episode:** the same rules as on iOS, as the player's round chip (`ChipButton`: 48 dp touch, 40 dp `scrim` disc, Material `skip_next` in `ink`) in the top app bar, before speed. TalkBack: "Next episode, S1 E2 · Northbound". The player looks the next episode up whether or not autoplay is on.

**Swipe for volume and brightness (Android, issue #40):** a vertical swipe on the right half sets the media volume (`STREAM_MUSIC`, without the system panel), on the left half this window's brightness (`screenBrightness`, never the system setting; back to `BRIGHTNESS_OVERRIDE_NONE` when the player closes). Up raises; a full sweep is 60% of the player's height. Swipes starting in the system gesture insets are ignored; taps, double taps and the hardware keys work as before, and a swipe neither shows nor hides the controls. While swiping, a vertical pill (44 × 152 dp, `scrim-strong` with the `progress-track`, an `ink` fill from the bottom, never copper) sits `space-6` in from the swiped edge with `volume_off` / `volume_down` / `volume_up` or `brightness_low` / `brightness_high` at its foot (`bg` once the fill covers it); it fades out 1 s after the finger lifts and TalkBack hears "Volume 60%". Off with the profile's "Swipe for volume and brightness" switch (on by default). Mockup: `PlayerGestures` in the design-system artifact.

**Cast (Android, planned, issue #48):** the top app bar's Cast button shows only when a device is found, and shows pressed while connected. It opens an M3 dialog on `surface-2`: "Cast to", the devices (cast glyph, name, type and state, a check on the connected one) and a "Stop casting" tonal button while connected. While casting, the player shows the same status pill as on iOS, "Casting to <device>" (cast glyph, `scrim-strong`, no glass), over the transport and scrubber on the item's artwork blurred and dimmed on the `scrim`, as on iOS. It stays while paused or stalled, the controls stay up, the volume and brightness swipes are off, and a long name is cut to one line. The Cast state is mocked up here; it is built with #48, not #47. The mockups are `PlayerControls`, `PlayerPhone` and `PlayerTablet` in the design-system artifact.

## Search for subtitles (issue #58)

The last row of the Subtitles list: a button with `magnifyingglass` at the end of the iOS `Menu`, a row with the search icon at the end of the Subtitles column of the Android track sheet. A title with no subtitles still has the list, with "Off" and this row. It shows under the same rules as on a title's page (`screens.md`), and not for what plays from a download: a new subtitle reaches streams only, not a title already on the device.

- **The sheet:** a native sheet with the title, a Language row, then the results. It searches straight away in the profile's subtitle language, or the device's when that has no preference. Grey lines while it loads.
- **Language:** the same chooser as Settings > Audio and subtitles (`PickerSheet`, `PickerSheetView`) without "Any language". A choice searches again for this search only; the profile's setting doesn't change.
- **Results:** hash matches first with an outline "Match" badge, then the highest rated. Name, then provider, format and rating. No copper.
- **Choosing one:** that row shows a spinner and the others are dimmed until the new track appears. Playback keeps going behind the sheet, and closing the sheet doesn't cancel the download. If it then fails with the sheet closed, the screen shows the message itself. Moving on to another episode closes the sheet and stops waiting for the old one's track.
- **Added:** the sheet closes, the track turns on at the current position and "English subtitles added" shows over the picture for 4 seconds. The player asks the server again, as for any subtitle it can't show by itself, so the stall spinner may show briefly; the position stays. On a title's page the message is the snackbar or banner, and the page reads the item again.
- **Nothing found:** the title says which language, with a hint to try another and that the server may need a subtitle provider plugin. A server can't say which of the two it is.
- **Errors:** no permission (a 403; the entry is hidden until the app next starts), no answer, with Try again, and a failed download, where the results stay and the message shows at the bottom. A 401 goes through `ConnectionManager.expireSession`, with no message.
- **A track that never arrives:** after 30 seconds the sheet says the subtitles didn't arrive and that the provider's daily limit may be reached. The server answers 204 either way, so this is the only sign of a refused download.

## Settings → Playback (per profile)

| Setting | Choices | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skip back | 5, 10, 15, 30 s | 10 s |
| Skip forward | 10, 15, 30, 45, 60 s | 10 s |
| Skip intros and recaps | on / off | off |
| Buffer ahead | Auto, 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60 s | 20 s |
| Still watching after | Off, 2 to 5 episodes | 3 |
| Quality | Maximum, 4K 40, 1080p 10, 720p 4, 480p 1.5 Mbps | Maximum |
| Swipe for volume and brightness | on / off | on |
| Subtitle style | system style, or size, text colour, background | system style |

Skip back and forward, auto skip, still watching and Buffer ahead are on the **Skipping and autoplay** page; Quality and Audio and subtitles (languages, then subtitle style) are their own pages under Playback. Quality caps the bitrate the server may send (`jellyfin-mapping.md`) and applies from the next stream; the player has no quality menu. Subtitle style follows the OS caption style until the user turns that off; Android applies the chosen size, colour and background to the subtitle view, iOS burns subtitles in today so its controls are disabled with a note.

Choices are the values SF Symbols has a `gobackward.<n>` / `goforward.<n>` glyph for, so button and setting agree. The values also drive the double-tap seek and the VoiceOver / TalkBack labels ("Back 10 seconds"). iOS: a `Picker` with `.pickerStyle(.menu)` per interval in the settings list; Android: the settings `ChoiceRow` (a menu with the value at the right), like the other choice settings. Buffer ahead sits in its own group on Skipping and autoplay, after the skip rows, with `Strings.bufferAheadHelp` as its help text: how much video the player loads ahead, a longer buffer riding out a slow connection at the cost of data and memory, Auto leaving it to the device's player. Auto is the platform player's own behaviour; a change applies from the next video.

## What changes in the code (for the plan)

- Shared: `ProfileSettings` (per profile) with skip back / forward and auto-skip, exposed on `PlaybackUiState`; `Strings` for the new labels. `PlaybackUiState.skipRun` (`SkipRun`: side, how far it moved, taps) adds up skips in a row and clears after `SKIP_RUN_WINDOW` (1 s).
- iOS: `PlayerView` top bar regrouped into the glass cluster, controls recoloured to `.primary`, transport gains the two skip buttons, skip offer and up next restyled.
- Android: transport gains the two skip buttons; top bar order as above.
- Both: segment skip already exists in the shared model; confirm it shows on the test server (an intro and outro segment fixture in `make-media.sh` if missing).
